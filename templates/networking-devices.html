Cables

  • Straight-through UTP: PC to switch, switch to router (different device types).
  • Crossover UTP: switch to switch, PC to PC, router to router (same device types). Modern ports use Auto-MDIX and often do not need it.
  • Fibre optic: long distance and high speed between buildings or floors.
  • Console cable: connects your computer to a router or switch for first-time setup.

In the simulator, any cable type works. Pay attention to which ports are shut down, because routers start with every interface administratively down.
