Private addresses are not routed on the internet. A router with NAT shares one public address among many private hosts. Other special addresses: 127.0.0.1 (loopback) and 169.254.x.x (APIPA, no DHCP answer).

Checklist when a ping fails

  1. Does the PC have the correct IP, mask and default gateway?
  2. Is the gateway inside the PC's own subnet, and is it the router's interface address?
  3. Are both ends of every cable up (no shutdown ports)?
  4. Does each router have a route to the destination and a route back to the source?
